These totals are not a per-code survival claim.

## Durable evidence and continuation

- `assessment.json`: follow-up seeds, actual budgets, complete final witness
  supports, bounded official audit excerpts and the superseded-gate warning.
- `screenings.json`: all 16 fresh exponent recipes and screening witnesses.
- `warm/results.json`: all 12 warm exponent recipes and screening witnesses.
- `campaign.json`, `journal.jsonl`, `manifest.json`, `summary.json`: the
  official campaign ledger for the initial fresh batch. The warm stage and
  later confirmations are recorded separately and are included in the total
  counts in `assessment.json`.
- `run.py`, `warm.py`: executable constructor/search code. Verification code
  was not edited. The source files were linted and formatted after execution;
  the warm manifest retains the executed and formatted script hashes.

The structured records retain the logical supports as well as the exponent
arrays, so no original scratch workspace is required to rebuild a matrix or
check a witness. Use `run.lift(array, P)` with the stored EX and EZ arrays.
The scripts refuse to overwrite an existing batch. To replay the search,
use a clean copy at the pinned upstream revision, copy the two scripts into
the same campaign directory, run `uv sync --frozen --extra research`, build
the unchanged accelerator with `CXX=g++ make fast`, and execute `run.py`
followed by `warm.py`. Replay in a separate checkout without these generated
batch records. RNG/accelerator results can vary with software/compiler
versions; the saved explicit operators remain independently checkable.

Next runs should avoid these two seeds and exact exponent hashes. Prefer a
different neighborhood or construction strategy; additional shallow CPM
headline bounds alone are not evidence of progress.
